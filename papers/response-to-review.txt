Response to the independent review — major-revision draft
4 October 2026

The English and Chinese manuscripts have been revised together. This reply
summarizes the mathematical clarifications, complete rule census and delivered
computational materials. The published version 1 and the frozen mathematical
checker sources remain unchanged. The review manuscripts and complete
certificate are supplied through the versioned GitHub release.

1. Mathematical framework.
   The insertion coverage, universal Black-outcome obligation, opening symmetry
   cover, and actual-placement clock are retained. In particular, the virtual
   occupancy argument and the treatment of unrecorded intermediate states in
   Lemmas 6.2 and 6.5 have not been shortened away.

2. Complete computational review materials.
   The release provides the original empty cover, the entire 1,482,906-file
   selected dependency closure, all eight frozen mathematical checker sources,
   the input byte manifest, licenses, archive checksums and verification entry.
   Original proof bytes and relative references are preserved. No proof file or
   mathematical checker was rewritten for packaging. The revised Section 10
   gives the download location, platform, exact command and successful output.
   Reviewers can run the complete mathematical check directly on these inputs;
   no engine, search cache or historical success receipt is required.
   The completed census measures 48,588,463,288 bytes of JSON dependencies and
   reproduces the 109,364,098 ordinary-node occurrence total.

3. Mathematical rule / program / certificate correspondence — completed.
   Appendix E lists all 17 accepted ordinary-node labels, mathematical results,
   checker entries, premises and actual-move clocks. Modular Black/White headers
   are identified separately. Theorem 6.7 and Theorem 6.8 are explicit rows.
   Usage is defined as root-reachable ordinary-node occurrences, summed over
   the 20 opening roots, rather than stored unreachable nodes or execution paths.
   All 17 rule counts are now bound to the complete selected corpus:
   12 labels occur and five have zero uses. Theorem 6.7 occurs 971,354 times,
   and Theorem 6.8 occurs 64,888 times. Their counts come from the full census,
   not samples. Independent CSV/JSON records give occurrence and distinct-path
   totals, with the 109,364,098 aggregate reproduced exactly.

4. Remote-interruption auditability — completed in the manuscript.
   Appendix F gives a complete small certificate with two active remote
   components, explicit points and gain/defense sets, local defense and
   counterfour handling, remote counterfours, quick finish, and an ordered
   strategy dispatcher. Both checkers reconstruct the complete conditions.
   It has 28 local recorded states and five states in each remote graph:
   L = 13, N1 = N2 = 1, hence at most 17 further actual placements, or 35
   including the 18 already placed. Complete state lists and a legal illustrative
   continuation are supplied separately; the path does not replace all-branch
   checking or contribute new opening coverage.

5. Completion-point lifting — completed.
   New Lemma 5.2 proves uniform emptiness and completion, independence of the
   supporting witness/reference, distinctness even across different rigid
   components, and inclusion of every actual immediate winning point, for both
   colors. The double-completion proof now invokes this lemma explicitly.

6. Related work — corrected.
   The introduction now reports both Allis et al.'s 35-ply principal variation
   and Table 5's solution-tree depth 35. It distinguishes their finite-board
   scope and threat compression from this paper's infinite-board bound on all
   actual placements. The limitation on applying the Infinite Hex locality
   result is retained.

7. Presentation and length — completed.
   The reviewer command is a literal one-line verbatim string, also delivered
   in verify-command.txt; PDF text extraction preserves both ASCII hyphens.
   Historical failures/cache qualifications are concentrated in Appendix D.
   Contributions/tool disclosure follows the already authorized public-v1
   disclosure and no longer awaits approval. Per-opening timing diagnostics
   move to opening-run-statistics.csv; the aggregate table and mathematical
   opening coverage remain in the manuscript. English remains 25 pages and
   Chinese 22 pages, with unchanged body font and margins.
